NotoSansTC-Regular.subset.otf is a glyph subset of Noto Sans CJK TC (Regular),
Copyright Google/Adobe, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org). It is embedded into the .pdf report export.
Subset ranges: Basic Latin, Latin-1, general punctuation, CJK symbols,
Bopomofo, CJK Unified Ideographs (U+4E00–U+9FFF), CJK compatibility forms,
halfwidth/fullwidth forms.
